### Ask NaijaMind AI Anything.

**NaijaMind AI** is a Nigerian-focused AI-powered platform built to help people ask questions, discover useful information, and find practical answers about Nigeria.

Unlike general-purpose AI assistants, NaijaMind AI is designed with **Nigeria at its core** — understanding Nigerian people, places, culture, businesses, services, transportation, education, government, entertainment, and everyday life.

## 💡 Our Vision

Our vision is simple:

> **Build an AI that understands Nigeria.**

Information about Nigeria is often scattered across different platforms, websites, social media, directories, and other sources. NaijaMind AI aims to bring relevant information together and make it easier for people to find, understand, and use.

## 🚀 What NaijaMind AI Can Help With

NaijaMind AI is being designed to support areas such as:

* 🤖 AI conversations and questions
* 🔎 Nigerian-focused search
* 📍 Nearby places and services
* 🚗 Transportation and routes
* 🏥 Hospitals and healthcare discovery
* 🎓 Schools and education
* 🏠 Housing and property information
* ⚖️ General Nigerian legal information
* 🏪 Markets and local services
* 🏨 Hotels, restaurants and entertainment
* 🏛️ Government information and services
* 📰 Current Nigerian information
* ✅ Claim and information verification
* 🎙️ Voice interaction
* 🇳🇬 Nigerian English and Pidgin understanding

## 🎯 Our Approach

NaijaMind AI is not intended to simply be another chatbot.

The long-term vision combines:

**AI + Nigerian Knowledge + Search + Local Discovery + Structured Data + Maps + Current Information**

This allows the platform to determine the appropriate information source depending on what a user is asking.

For example:

> "How I fit reach Ikeja from Yaba?"

The system can combine transportation and location information to provide a practical answer.

Or:

> "Which hospital near me has oxygen?"

The platform can use location and healthcare information to help the user find relevant options.

## 🔐 Trust & Accuracy

Trust is a core part of NaijaMind AI.

The platform is designed to distinguish between information that is:

* **Verified**
* **Current**
* **Estimated**
* **User Reported**
* **Unverified**
* **Outdated**

NaijaMind AI should never pretend that uncertain information is confirmed.

For time-sensitive information such as prices, transport fares, government officials, news, or availability, sources and update times should be provided whenever possible.
